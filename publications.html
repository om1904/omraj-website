Assessment of Physicochemical Parameters of Water Samples from the Ganga River at Patna

Raj, O., & Ambreen, D. (2026)

International Journal of Versatile Research and Analysis (IJVRA), Volume 4, Issue 3, Pages 784–810. ISSN: 2984-8903. Published Paper ID: IJVRA2603368

Abstract: This research paper delivers a comprehensive multi-station spatial assessment of key physicochemical water parameters across urban ghats of the Ganga River in Patna. Water samples were analyzed for temperature, pH, electrical conductivity, dissolved oxygen (DO), biochemical oxygen demand ($\text{BOD}_5$), chemical oxygen demand (COD), total dissolved solids (TDS), total hardness, alkalinity, and microbiological contamination. Findings were evaluated against CPCB, WHO, and BIS 10500 standards to determine the impact of untreated municipal sewage discharge on riverine water safety and aquatic ecosystem sustainability.

Comparative Assessment of Pineapple Peel, Jackfruit Peel, and Drek Wood Biochar on Plant Growth, Soil Fertility, and Biochemical Responses

Role: Researcher | Institution: Indian Institute of Technology Ropar (IIT Ropar) | Supervisor: Dr. Indramani Dhada | Collaborator: Mohammad Mustafa (International Research Student, IIT Ropar)

Study Description: Conducting systematic research evaluating biochar produced from agricultural waste (pineapple peel, jackfruit peel) and forestry waste (Drek wood) feedstocks. Assessing seed germination rates, root/shoot length, biomass accumulation, soil pH, electrical conductivity, available nitrogen, phosphorus, potassium (NPK), and soil organic carbon. Analyzing plant biochemical stress responses including total protein, total soluble sugars, proline, malondialdehyde (MDA), and antioxidant enzymes (Superoxide Dismutase - SOD, Catalase - CAT, Peroxidase - POD, and total phenolics). Performing ANOVA and post-hoc Tukey tests across biochar application rates.

Spatiotemporal Assessment Of Ambient Air Quality In Selected Urban, Tourist And Industrial Regions Of Himachal Pradesh, India: A Case Study

OM RAJ, Indramani Dhada

Preprint platform: Research Square | DOI: 10.21203/rs.3.rs-10492149/v1

Abstract Summary: Investigating ambient air quality fluctuations ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$, $\text{PM}_{10}$, $\text{SO}_2$, $\text{NO}_x$, $\text{CO}$) across tourist hubs, urban towns, and industrial clusters in Himachal Pradesh. Employs spatiotemporal modeling and Air Quality Index (AQI) calculations to assess industrial compliance and seasonal tourism impacts on ambient air toxicity.
