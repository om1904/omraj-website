IIT Ropar | Department of Civil Engineering PPCB Sponsored Project

Wastewater Management & Pollutant Dynamics Project

Institution: Indian Institute of Technology Ropar (IIT Ropar) | Project In-charge / Supervisors: Prof. Indramani Dhada, Prof. Neelkanth Nirmalkar | Project Manager: Dr. Harsh Sharma

This project focused on comprehensive water sampling and laboratory analysis across industrial and municipal wastewater treatment facilities, as well as natural aquatic receivers in Punjab.

Core Methodologies & Key Tasks:

  • Field Sampling Protocols: Systematic sampling from Common Effluent Treatment Plants (CETP), Sewage Treatment Plants (STP), Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP), and the Sutlej River.
  • Physicochemical Testing: Real-time estimation using multimeters, DO meters, pH meters; titrations for alkalinity, chloride, and total hardness.
  • Pollutant & Metal Quantification: Estimation of Total Suspended Solids (TSS), Biochemical Oxygen Demand ($\text{BOD}_5$), Chemical Oxygen Demand (COD), Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Total Coliform, Sulfide, Oil & Grease, Phenols, and Total Chromium.
  • Laboratory Protocols & SOPs: Reagent preparation, spectrophotometer calibration, quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC), and data management.

IIT Ropar | Punjab State Government Project Excise & Taxation Dept. Sponsor

Audit & Technical Assessment of Breweries, Distilleries, and Bottling Plants across Punjab

Principal Investigator: Dr. Indramani Dhada (Assistant Professor, Civil Engineering Dept., IIT Ropar) | Sponsor: Excise and Taxation Department, Government of Punjab

Executed state-wide environmental compliance audits and technical process evaluations of beverage and industrial fermentation plants across Punjab to enforce regulatory discharge standards.

Core Responsibilities & Technical Deliverables:

  • Industrial On-Site Inspections: Audit of industrial water consumption, raw water treatment units, fermentation effluent streams, and zero liquid discharge (ZLD) systems.
  • Wastewater Effluent Sampling: Collection and lab analysis of high-COD brewery/distillery effluent, wash waters, and treated discharge.
  • Environmental Compliance Evaluation: Assessing facility compliance against Punjab Pollution Control Board (PPCB) and Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB) norms.
  • Technical Reporting: Data interpretation, statistical synthesis, and preparation of official audit reports submitted to government regulatory authorities.

Patna University | M.Sc. Master Thesis M.Sc. Dissertation

Water Quality Monitoring of the Ganga River at Patna Ghats Using Multi-Parameter Analysis

Institution: Department of Botany, Patna University | Supervisor: Prof. Dr. Meenakshi Singh (Head, Department of Biotechnology, Patna University)

Investigative research analyzing spatial variations in river water quality parameters across major urban ghats of the Ganga River in Patna subjected to municipal sewage discharge.

Key Highlights & Results:

  • Multi-station spatial sampling across key ghats (Bhadra Ghat, Gandhi Ghat, Collectorate Ghat, Digha Ghat).
  • Physicochemical characterization: pH, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), $\text{BOD}_5$, COD, TDS, electrical conductivity, chloride, total alkalinity, and total microbial coliform counts.
  • Comparative benchmark analysis against CPCB (Class A/B/C/D), WHO, and Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS 10500) drinking and bathing criteria.
