Academic Biography

About Om Raj & Research Vision

Emerging Environmental Scientist & Researcher in Civil Engineering at IIT Ropar specializing in aquatic pollutant dynamics, plant genomics, biochar remediation, and scientific software engineering.

Career Objective & Profile

I am an M.Sc. Botany graduate (7.68 CGPA) and Emerging Environmental Scientist with interdisciplinary research experience spanning environmental biotechnology, water and wastewater management, ambient air quality assessment, plant science, molecular biology, plant genomics, and environmental data analytics. Currently engaged in research activities within the Department of Civil Engineering at IIT Ropar under Dr. Indramani Dhada, contributing to CETP, STP, ETP, industrial audits, and Sutlej River system evaluations.

Interdisciplinary Philosophy

Modern environmental science demands a convergence of botanical genomics, environmental engineering, and custom software tools. By pairing lab assays (SOD, CAT, POD antioxidant enzymes, proline, MDA, plant tissue culture at JNU) with self-developed software ecosystems (SNIFF DOI 2.0 and WASH v2.0), my goal is to drive reproducible green remediation and sustainable water management solutions.
